\section*{Roadmap}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Roadmap}
\noindent\revs{Section~\ref{sec:covariance} characterizes covariance matrices by the matrix
inequality~\eqref{eq:admissibility} and proves that symplectic congruence, the selection of a
subsystem and independent sums preserve it. Section~\ref{sec:states} constructs normalized
Gaussian wave functions and computes their covariances, which realize the equality case of the
one-mode criterion of Proposition~\ref{prop:diagonal}. Section~\ref{sec:contraction} defines the
contraction of covariance matrices, a Schur complement motivated by EPR measurements on virtual
modes, proves that it preserves admissibility, and assembles from it the covariance of a periodic
Gaussian matrix product state. Sections~\ref{sec:fourier} and~\ref{sec:products} treat two cases
of that construction: chains with the same local covariance at every site, whose covariance splits into one
$2\times2$ block per frequency, and products.}

\medskip
\noindent\revs{Section~\ref{sec:fock} uses none of Sections~\ref{sec:covariance}--\ref{sec:products}.
From algebra and formal power series alone it builds the formal Fock space of all coefficient
families over the number basis, its ladder operators, the exponentials of operators that raise the
particle number, and the Bargmann picture; it is the foundation of the Gaussian-augmented bosonic
matrix product states of~\cite{tjoacirac}.} \vetb{Section~\ref{sec:fockspace} places the bosonic
Fock space inside the formal one, realizes coefficient-finite maps as closed operators and their sums
of squared norms as closed forms, and proves the invariance of the photon-added class.}
\vetd{Section~\ref{sec:secondquant} defines the second quantization $\Gamma(g)$ of every complex matrix on the formal
Fock space, shows that the unitary matrices give a strongly continuous unitary representation on the bosonic Fock
space, and identifies $\Gamma(e^h)$ with the coefficientwise exponential of $d\Gamma(h)$. Section~\ref{sec:blochmessiah}
uses only the symplectic matrices of Section~\ref{sec:covariance}: it identifies the orthogonal symplectic matrices
with the unitary matrices and proves the Bloch--Messiah and polar decompositions.}
\vete{Section~\ref{sec:displacement} constructs the displacement and squeezing operators on the bosonic Fock space from
their normal-ordered forms, through the exponential vectors, and proves the irreducibility of the displacements.
Section~\ref{sec:gaussunitary} defines Gaussian unitaries by their action on the Weyl operators and factors each into
passive, displacement and squeezing unitaries, using the Bloch--Messiah decomposition. Section~\ref{sec:puregaussian}
proves that the pure Gaussian states, the vacuum vectors of the Gaussian unitaries, are exactly the vectors with a
Gaussian characteristic function.}
\vetr{Section~\ref{sec:quadratic} proves that $e^{-itH}$ is a Gaussian unitary for every quadratic Hamiltonian $H=\frac12\hat R^TK\hat R+f^T\hat R$, with symplectic matrix $e^{t\Omega K}$; it first develops the one-parameter unitary groups and their generators that the statement needs.}
