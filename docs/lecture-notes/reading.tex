\section*{Reading guide}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Reading guide}
\revb{These notes state their definitions and theorems in forms that a proof assistant can check;
the remarks between them explain. Where that form differs from the usual physics presentation,
the table below names the object used here, the one used in the literature, and where the two
are related.}
